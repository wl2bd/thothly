# Layout concepts

Static mockups made for the redesign (September 2026). They are not wired to anything, and they stay here to record the choice.

The problem: the app opened on a landing page, then switched into the tool. That sequence felt wrong.

| Route | Concept | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/concepts/a` | Tool first: compact result list, source bar at the bottom | Liked, but the compilation is reduced to a bar |
| `/concepts/b` | Two panes: search on the left, the compilation built live on the right, no separate Review step | Strong, but the thumbnails are too big |
| `/concepts/c` | The book is the canvas: table of contents in the middle, search in a Ctrl K palette | Search becomes secondary |
| `/concepts/d` | B with A's compact rows | Chosen |

## Why D

Adding a result shows it in the compilation right away, and nothing has to open for that to happen. The compilation never disappears behind the search. It keeps B's structure and A's density.

## Next
